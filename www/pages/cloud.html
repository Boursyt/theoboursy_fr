theoboursy cloud

theoboursy cloud is a private Nextcloud instance run by Théo Boursy on his own servers in France, for himself and a few people he invites.

# What it does

  • store, sync and share files
  • calendars, contacts and notes
  • edit documents, spreadsheets and presentations in the browser

# Who can use it

Invitation only: an account must be created by the administrator before its owner can sign in. There is no public sign-up.

# Signing in

Sign-in uses Sign in with Google, so no password is stored on this service. It only asks Google for your name, email address and profile picture, to identify your account. See the privacy policy.
