Privacy policy — theoboursy cloud

This policy covers theoboursy cloud (cloud.lab.pub.theoboursy.fr), a private Nextcloud instance. Last update: November 2026.

# Data controller

Théo Boursy, as an individual — boursyt@gmail.com.

# Data received from Google

When you choose Sign in with Google, the service receives from Google only your name, email address and profile picture (scopes openid, email, profile). They are used only to sign you in and to show your name in the service. No other Google data (Drive, Gmail, contacts…) is accessed.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

# Data you store

Files, calendars, contacts and other content you add are stored on the administrator's own servers in France. Encrypted backups are kept in Google Cloud Storage in the European Union (Paris region).

# Sharing

Your data is never sold, shared or used for advertising. There are no analytics and no trackers. Only strictly necessary session cookies are used.

# Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account exists. Ask for deletion at any time by email: your account and content are deleted within 30 days, and disappear from rotating backups within 12 months.

# Your rights

Under the GDPR you can access, correct, export or delete your data by writing to the address above. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL.
